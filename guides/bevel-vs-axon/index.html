Bevel vs Axon

Bevel vs Axon is a comparison between two different shapes of app. Bevel is a broad AI health coach that covers exercise, sleep and nutrition and works with or without an Apple Watch. Axon is narrow: four on-device recovery scores, Apple Watch required, free.

By the Axon team · Updated 12 September 2026 · Prices are US App Store, September 2026

Quick answer. Bevel gives you nutrition tracking, an AI coach and multi-device import (Garmin, Oura, WHOOP) without needing an Apple Watch, for a Pro price of $14.99/month or $99.99/year plus optional credit packs. Axon computes four daily scores — Sleep, Recovery, Strain, Readiness — and a Fit Age reading, entirely on-device from an Apple Watch, for free, with no nutrition tracking and no AI coach. Pick Bevel for breadth and device flexibility; pick Axon for a free, focused, Watch-first read of your recovery.

Scope: broad coach vs narrow scores

Bevel's subtitle is "Exercise, Sleep & Nutrition" — it's built as an AI health coach across three domains, not just a recovery reading. It works without an Apple Watch and, per its 2026 App Store reviews, imports data from Garmin, Oura and WHOOP, which makes it usable for people who already track on a different device.

Axon does one thing: four daily scores computed from Apple Watch and iPhone sensor data, plus a Fit Age reading derived from cardio fitness trend. Readiness is a live "bio-recharge" arc — an overnight anchor weighted 40% from Sleep plus Recovery and activity balance, drained through the day by strain and topped up by rest. Recovery blends HRV and resting heart rate against your own 30-day baseline with sleep quality, respiratory rate and blood oxygen as smaller contributors. There is no nutrition tracker and no AI coach — Axon shows the scores and the reasoning behind them, and stops there.

Price, and the "free" confusion worth naming directly

Bevel has a free tier and a Pro trial. Pro costs $14.99/month or $99.99/year, and Bevel also sells "Intelligence Credits" in packs from $4.99 to $49.99 for its AI features. Some 1-star reviews on the App Store describe ads calling the app free, with the app itself opening on a 7-day trial that converted to the $14.99/month charge once it ended. We're not repeating that to score a point — subscription trials that auto-convert are common across the App Store, and the fix is simply to check the trial terms in Bevel's own paywall before you start one.

Axon's four scores and Fit Age are free, with no trial and nothing that converts to a charge. Axon's code contains a dormant premium tier, but it is switched off — nothing in the app is metered or paywalled today.

Works without a Watch, and imports other ecosystems

This is Bevel's clearest structural advantage. It's designed to work without an Apple Watch at all, and per its 2026 reviews it can import data from Garmin, Oura and WHOOP — useful if you're not all-in on Apple's ecosystem or you're switching devices and don't want to lose history. Axon has no import path and no non-Watch mode: Recovery, Strain and Readiness need an Apple Watch (Series 4 or later, SE, or Ultra; SpO2 and wrist-temperature rows need Series 6+/8+), and there's no way around that requirement.

Platforms

Bevel requires iOS 18 or later. Axon requires iOS 17+ with a paired Apple Watch, and adds Lock Screen and Home Screen widgets plus a Watch face complication so a score is visible without opening the app.

Privacy and on-device scoring

Axon computes its scores on the iPhone itself; health data is not uploaded anywhere by default, and no account is required to start. We don't have verified detail on how Bevel processes data server-side for its AI coach — an AI coaching feature typically needs some server-side processing to function, so if on-device-only matters to you, check Bevel's own privacy policy rather than assuming either way.

Data honesty

Axon's rule is "real data or nothing": a missing reading shows a dash and the reason, never a filled-in number. We can speak to that because it's our own app's design commitment; we have no verified basis for comparing it to how Bevel handles missing or imported data, so we won't guess.

Pick Bevel if…

  • You want nutrition tracking and an AI coach alongside recovery, not just scores.
  • You don't own an Apple Watch, or you already track on Garmin, Oura or WHOOP and want that history imported.
  • You're comfortable evaluating a subscription trial and reading its terms before it converts.

Pick Axon if…

  • You want four on-device recovery scores for free, with nothing to subscribe to or cancel.
  • You own an Apple Watch and want your existing sensor data turned into a daily readiness picture, nothing more.
  • You'd rather not hand nutrition or AI-coaching data to a third service you don't need.

Who should NOT pick Axon

If you don't own an Apple Watch, Axon has little to offer you — Recovery, Strain and Readiness simply need one, and there's no import path around that. If you're already invested in Garmin, Oura or WHOOP and want that history carried forward, Axon can't import it; Bevel can. And if what you actually want is nutrition tracking or an AI coach that talks back, that's Bevel's whole reason for existing and it's not something Axon does at all. In every one of these cases, Bevel's broader scope is the better fit, even accounting for its Pro price.

FAQ

What's the core difference between Bevel and Axon?

Bevel is broad: exercise, sleep and nutrition tracking with an AI coach, and it works without an Apple Watch by importing data from Garmin, Oura or WHOOP. Axon is narrow: four scores (Sleep, Recovery, Strain, Readiness) plus Fit Age, computed on-device from an Apple Watch, free, no nutrition tracking or AI coach.

Is Bevel actually free?

Bevel has a free tier and a Pro trial. Pro runs $14.99/month or $99.99/year, with additional "Intelligence Credits" packs from $4.99 to $49.99. Some 1-star App Store reviews say ads described the app as free and it opened on a 7-day trial that converted to the $14.99/month charge — read the trial terms before you start one.

Does Bevel work without an Apple Watch?

Yes. Bevel is built to work without a Watch and can import data from Garmin, Oura and WHOOP. Axon cannot do this — Recovery, Strain and Readiness need an Apple Watch.

Can Bevel import my Garmin, Oura or WHOOP data?

Per its 2026 App Store reviews, yes, Bevel imports data from Garmin, Oura and WHOOP. Axon reads only Apple Health / Apple Watch data and does not import from other ecosystems.

Is Axon's free tier really free, with no trial?

Yes. Axon's four scores and Fit Age are free with no trial period and no credit card required to start. There is no subscription currently active in the app.

Which app should I pick if I don't own an Apple Watch?

Bevel. Axon's Recovery, Strain and Readiness scores require an Apple Watch; without one, Axon has nothing meaningful to show you. Bevel is designed to work without a Watch.
