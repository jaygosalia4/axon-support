Connect Axon to your AI assistant

Ask Claude, ChatGPT or any assistant about your sleep, recovery and food, and get answers from the scores Axon already computes from your Apple Watch.

By the Axon team · Updated 11 October 2026

The promise, in plain words. Your health data never leaves your phone — unless you say so. Switching this on says so: Axon keeps a copy of your scores and daily summaries so the assistant you choose can read it with your key. Switching it off deletes the copy. Nothing happens until you turn it on.

Before you start

  1. Update Axon to 2.1 on your iPhone.
  2. Open Profile → Connections → Connect Axon to my AI assistant and tap Turn on.
  3. Axon shows your key once. Copy it — it starts with axon_ — or tap Copy everything for your assistant for one message with the key and every address. Lost it? Make a new key on the same page; the old one stops working.
  4. Pick your assistant below. Axon's own page walks the same steps, with copy buttons.

Claude Code (on your Mac)

Paste this into Terminal, with your key in place of axon_…, and press Return:

claude mcp add --scope user --transport http axon https://qnpnvdainbakygnnktrn.supabase.co/functions/v1/ai/mcp --header "Authorization: Bearer axon_…"

Then open Claude Code and ask how you slept this week. --scope user makes Axon available in every folder you open; without it, Claude Code keeps it for the current folder only. The command saves your key in Claude Code's own settings on that Mac.

Claude Desktop, claude.ai and the Claude iPhone app add connectors with a sign-in that Axon doesn't offer yet. For now, Claude Code is the Claude that connects.

ChatGPT (a GPT of your own)

  1. On chatgpt.com, create a GPT and open Configure.
  2. Under Actions, create a new action and import from this address:
    https://qnpnvdainbakygnnktrn.supabase.co/functions/v1/ai/v1/openapi.json
  3. Set Authentication to API Key, auth type Bearer, and paste your key. Save.

GPTs are made on chatgpt.com, not inside the ChatGPT iPhone app; once saved, you can use yours anywhere you use ChatGPT.

Any assistant

One paste. On the key page (or Any assistant in Axon), tap Copy everything and paste it into the assistant's chat. It carries everything an assistant needs to connect on its own:

Connect to Axon, my health app. It has an API you can read and write through.
REST address: https://qnpnvdainbakygnnktrn.supabase.co/functions/v1/ai/v1
API description (OpenAPI): https://qnpnvdainbakygnnktrn.supabase.co/functions/v1/ai/v1/openapi.json
MCP address: https://qnpnvdainbakygnnktrn.supabase.co/functions/v1/ai/mcp
Authorization: Bearer axon_…
How it works: https://jaygosalia4.github.io/axon-support/ai/

Then ask how you slept this week. Anything that speaks MCP or plain HTTP can connect this way. Send your key as Authorization: Bearer axon_… on every request.

  • MCP (streamable HTTP): https://qnpnvdainbakygnnktrn.supabase.co/functions/v1/ai/mcp
  • REST: https://qnpnvdainbakygnnktrn.supabase.co/functions/v1/ai/v1 — every tool and its words are listed in the OpenAPI document.
curl -H "Authorization: Bearer axon_…" https://qnpnvdainbakygnnktrn.supabase.co/functions/v1/ai/v1/today

What your assistant can read and log

  • Reads: today, any day or range, last night's sleep, trends, Fit Age, food, check-ins, warnings and training load — in the words Axon shows you, with a missing reading marked missing, never filled with a number.
  • Logs, if you allow it: a mood check-in, a meal or a nap. Each appears in Axon the next time it updates. Switch Let it log for me off to keep it read only.

What leaves your iPhone, and what never does

  • Leaves, only while it's on: your scores and what drives them; sleep with bed and wake times and naps; heart, breathing rate, blood oxygen and wrist temperature; workouts, steps, energy, walking, daylight and sound levels; check-ins, food you logged in Axon, Fit Age, warnings and trends; your age, sex, height, weight, units and time zone. The last 120 days, then each new day.
  • Never leaves: raw Apple Health samples, your journal's words, your calendar, your name and your Apple ID. The copy is kept under a random ID.
  • Who can read it: an assistant only with your key. Axon's team can reach it to keep the service running, never for anything else. What your assistant's provider does with what it reads is covered by that provider's privacy policy.
  • Off deletes it: turning the connection off ends the key and deletes the copy and every pending request in one step. If your iPhone doesn't update it for 45 days, it is deleted by itself.

The full detail is section 3a of the privacy policy.

Ask Siri BETA

Siri and the Shortcuts app answer from Axon on your iPhone — nothing is sent anywhere. The full list is in Profile → Connections → Ask Axon with Siri. Say:

  • "How am I doing in Axon" — all four scores and the day's note
  • "What is my readiness in Axon" — one score and what drives it
  • "How did I sleep in Axon" · "Explain my readiness in Axon"
  • "Log my mood in Axon" · "Log a nap in Axon"
  • "Copy my health brief from Axon" — the last 7 days, to paste into any chat

Ask without the app name. Siri runs a shortcut by its name. In Axon, open Profile → Connections → Ask Axon with Siri and tap Add to Siri beside a question like "How am I doing today": Axon opens Shortcuts and walks you through three steps, once.

Does my health data leave my iPhone?

Only if you switch the connection on in Profile → Connections. Then Axon keeps a copy of your scores and daily summaries — never raw Apple Health samples, your journal or your calendar — under a random ID, so your assistant can read it with your key. Turning it off deletes the copy.

Can my assistant change my data?

It can ask Axon to log a mood check-in, a meal or a nap, the same entries you make by hand. Each one appears the next time Axon updates on your iPhone. You can switch logging off and keep the connection read only.

What if I lose the key?

Make a new key on the same page in Axon. The old one stops working at once, and your assistant needs the new one.
